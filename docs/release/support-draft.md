## Photo access

Stillmint asks for access after you choose to continue. With Limited access it reviews the media you select. Use Settings → Manage Photo Access in the app, or iOS Settings → Apps → Stillmint → Photos (labels vary by iOS version), to adjust access. Full access permits a broader review, not a guarantee that all matches will be found. If an active album rule cannot be verified, cleanup pauses rather than guessing membership. Resolve access or explicitly change the rule and start a fresh review.

## Similar photos and keepers

Nothing is selected for deletion by default. Open a photo or A/B comparison and inspect dates, dimensions and estimated size. Keeper suggestions are not quality guarantees. Choosing a different keeper does not select another item for deletion. Protect anything important with Skip & protect, album or recent-media rules. Favorited/edited media also has protection. If the library or rules change, old selections may be cleared for safety.

## Compression and originals

Choose a profile and create a free short sample. Sample size measures only the sample, not promised whole-video savings. Full compression saves a separate copy; Keep both is the default. Formats and available temporary storage can limit export, even with Lifetime. Compression is not lossless and a smaller output is not guaranteed. For unavailable iCloud media, make it available through Photos and try again.

### A saved copy is awaiting verification

Use Retry saved-copy verification for that existing operation. A copy may already exist in Photos; do not repeatedly create new copies to resolve an unclear save. Pending verification does not use successful compression allowance and does not authorize original cleanup. If permission is Limited or the exact output cannot be accessed, keep the original and resolve access. If Full access confirms the exact saved output is absent, the app can close that failed operation and allow a new export. An unknown save without a recorded output must remain conservative; contact **TBD** if it does not resolve. Never delete an important original based on an unverified output.

## Deletion and Recently Deleted

Review the summary before Apple's confirmation. Canceling keeps items unchanged and does not consume confirmed-deletion allowance. Partial results list confirmed deletions and unchanged items. For recovery, open Photos, locate Recently Deleted, unlock if required and restore items Apple still retains. Navigation varies by iOS. Stillmint cannot recover items permanently deleted from Photos and does not maintain its own permanent backup. Keep independent backups.

If a cleanup was interrupted, its final result may be unverified. Missing items alone do not prove that Stillmint deleted them. Do not repeat deletion to settle the record. Follow the in-app verification instructions; closing an unverified record is not a media restore or a reset of previously confirmed usage.

## Free allowances and payment

Free use is a lifetime total of 100 confirmed ordinary deletions and 10 successfully saved, verified full compressions; no daily reset. Samples, failed/canceled exports and pending verification do not consume successful compression uses. Clearing Activity does not reset allowances. The exact original of a verified saved compressed copy can be exempt from ordinary deletion allowance, but only after revalidation and normal protection/confirmation.

Lifetime is one-time, not a subscription. Final price is shown by the App Store; this draft does not set a price. Use Restore Purchases in Settings or the purchase screen with the same Apple Account. Check your connection and Apple Account if products do not load. A pending/failed/unverified transaction does not unlock features. For refunds use Apple's purchase-support routes; do not send card details to Stillmint. **The release owner must complete purchase/restore/refund Sandbox acceptance before this page is published for a released build.**
