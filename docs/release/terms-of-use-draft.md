## Using Stillmint

Stillmint is an iPhone utility for reviewing authorized photos/videos and saving smaller video copies. You decide what to keep or remove. Matching is a suggestion, not a guarantee that two pictures are identical, that a keeper is the best shot, or that every duplicate has been found. File sizes can be estimates; smaller media files do not necessarily equal increased available device space.

Keep independent backups of important media. Deletions are performed through Apple Photos after your confirmation. Recovery depends on items still being retained by Photos; Stillmint does not promise permanent backup, guaranteed recovery or a separate recycle bin. Compression is not lossless, depends on supported formats and temporary storage, and may produce no savings. Originals are kept by default; deleting an original is a separate explicit action.

Free use includes a lifetime total of 100 confirmed ordinary deletions and a separate 10 successfully saved and verified full video compressions. No daily reset or ordinary-deletion byte cap applies. Failed, canceled or unverified operations do not count as successful use; samples do not use full-export allowance. Each distinct verified compression job counts once, including a new job for the same source, while retries of the same operation do not count twice.

A verified compressed-source cleanup may be exempt from ordinary deletion allowance only while its exact saved output can be verified. Protection and Photos confirmation always apply.
