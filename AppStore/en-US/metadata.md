## Description

Make space without guessing.

Stillmint helps you review photos and videos that may be taking up space on your iPhone. See candidates, compare photos, preview media and choose exactly what to remove.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU DECIDE

• Review exact-duplicate and close-capture similar-photo candidates
• Gather screenshots, screen recordings and large videos
• Compare two photos with dates, dimensions and estimated sizes
• Choose a preferred keeper without automatically selecting another photo for deletion
• Work in 20-, 50- or 100-item review batches and resume saved progress

PROTECT WHAT MATTERS

Nothing is selected by default. Protect individual items, albums and recent media. Favorited and edited items have additional protection. If active album protection cannot be verified with your current Photos access, cleanup pauses until the rule can be checked or you explicitly change it. Review your selection, then confirm deletion with Apple Photos.

SMALLER VIDEO COPIES, ORIGINALS KEPT

Create a short sample before a full export. Choose a quality profile, then save a separate compressed copy. A saved output must be checked before completion is reported. The default is Keep both; originals are never deleted automatically. Compression is not lossless and may not reduce every file. A smaller copy does not itself mean more device storage is available while the original remains.

FREE SAFETY, ONE-TIME UPGRADE

Scanning, recommendations, previews, comparisons, protection, review progress, Activity and recovery guidance are free. Free use includes a lifetime total of 100 confirmed ordinary deletions with no byte cap and 10 successfully saved, verified full video compressions. These allowances do not reset daily. Failed or canceled operations do not consume successful-use allowance; outputs awaiting verification do not consume compression use. Preview samples do not consume export allowance. Each distinct verified export counts once; retrying the same operation does not count again.

One optional Lifetime Unlock purchase enables unlimited eligible cleanup and full video compression. No subscription or recurring charge. The localized price is shown by the App Store before purchase. Device format support, temporary storage and protection rules still apply to free and paid use.

Reviewing the exact original associated with a verified compressed copy does not use ordinary deletion allowance when the saved copy can still be verified. Protection and explicit Photos confirmation always apply.

PRIVATE, ON-DEVICE REVIEW

Photo analysis runs on your iPhone. Stillmint does not upload media to a developer service, use third-party advertising, or require an account. Apple Photos may download your own iCloud media when you request a full preview. Local Activity distinguishes confirmed counts from estimated sizes; it does not claim estimated file sizes equal measured device space freed.

DELETION AND RECOVERY

Deleted items may remain in Recently Deleted in Photos, subject to Apple's retention and your actions. Find Recently Deleted in Photos to recover items Apple still retains. Stillmint does not provide a permanent backup or one-tap rollback. It does not clean RAM, iOS caches or other apps' files. Matching and availability depend on the authorized library; no perfect-match or all-duplicates-found guarantee is made.
